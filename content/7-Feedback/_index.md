Concluding the 12-week journey with the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program organized by **AWS Vietnam**, I would like to offer my personal reflections and constructive suggestions to the Organizers (BTC). These insights are derived from genuine internship experiences, aiming to contribute value toward refining and elevating the program quality for future cohorts.

### OVERALL PROGRAM EVALUATION

**1. Learning and Research Environment**  
The online learning infrastructure was thoughtfully structured, featuring intuitive AWS Study Group lab modules that were logically arranged and easy to navigate. Interactive channels between learners and administrators remained consistently open and supportive. Nevertheless, because of the large cohort size relative to office space constraints, a subset of students faced challenges in fulfilling the recommended quota for in-person office visits and physical events.

**2. Support from Mentors and Admin Team**  
I am deeply impressed by the technical expertise and active guidance provided by the Mentors. Instead of serving immediate answers, they cultivated architectural problem-solving mindsets—encouraging students to inspect Amazon CloudWatch logs independently and resolve JVM cold start or configuration issues. Concurrently, the Admin team provided prompt assistance regarding AWS Budgets credit allocations, ensuring uninterrupted hands-on practice.

**3. Alignment Between Training Content and Academic Major**  
The curriculum proved exceptionally relevant to my Software Engineering coursework. Bridging fundamental Cloud infrastructure topics (IAM, ECR, Lambda, VPC) with advanced Spring Boot 3 Serverless deployment strategies (`aws-serverless-java-container-springboot3`, Shaded JARs) provided a holistic perspective—spanning local development loops to production-grade AWS releases.

**4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities**  
The program accelerated both my technical competencies and professional soft skills. On the technical side, I achieved working proficiency in configuring IAM Profiles, crafting Multi-stage Docker builds, configuring `maven-shade-plugin`, and launching Spring Boot 3 Serverless FaaS modules via the AWS Lambda Console. Regarding soft skills, my capacity for self-directed documentation research, Git/GitHub source management, and technical writing improved noticeably.

**5. Culture and Team Spirit**  
FCAJ successfully established a culture centered around self-driven learning and open knowledge sharing. Team cohesion peaked during intense debugging sprints—such as resolving Shaded JAR archive errors or handling Binary Stream payload mismatches—where peers readily assisted one another. Conquering weekly hands-on labs collaboratively fostered a strong sense of community.

**6. Support Policies and Privileges**  
Full financial sponsorship of AWS consumption costs during lab exercises represented an invaluable advantage. Furthermore, access to AWS Certified exam vouchers alongside standard official learning paths offered immense added value, inspiring students to pursue long-term Cloud engineering careers.

### FEEDBACK ON SURVEY QUESTIONS

*   **What satisfied you the most during the program?**  
    The 100% hands-on approach stood out the most. Rather than remaining purely theoretical, students gained full Console access to containerize Spring Boot 3 applications with Maven/Docker and directly execute serverless workloads on AWS Lambda.
*   **What do you think the program should improve for future cohorts?**  
    The organizers might consider hosting dedicated deep-dive technical workshops on mitigating Java Cold Start latency on AWS Lambda, alongside presenting real-world enterprise case studies on migrating legacy Monolithic architectures to Serverless setups.
*   **If recommending to friends, would you advise them to join this program? Why?**  
    Without hesitation, yes. It provides an exceptional foundation for any IT student seeking to master Cloud Computing principles and build competitive practical experience prior to graduation.

### RECOMMENDATIONS AND FUTURE ASPIRATIONS

*   **Improvement Recommendation:** I propose creating a centralized Knowledge Base Bug Tracker documenting frequent lab pitfalls (e.g., IAM permission errors, Shaded JAR configuration flaws, or Request/Response Stream handler issues). This would allow upcoming students to troubleshoot independently, preventing WhatsApp message overload and saving Mentor bandwidth.
*   **Future Aspirations:** I hope to remain engaged with the AWS Vietnam ecosystem moving forward, whether through advanced Solutions Architect training initiatives or internship/career opportunities within AWS partner networks.
