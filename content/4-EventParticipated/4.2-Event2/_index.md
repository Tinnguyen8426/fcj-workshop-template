# Summary Report: "Swinburne Vietnam IT Students Explore Career Opportunities in Cloud Computing at AWS Vietnam"

### Event Purpose
- Visit and learn directly at the office of Amazon Web Services (AWS) Vietnam.
- Explore career opportunities, growth trends, and prospects in Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Access a professional working environment, reinforce specialized knowledge, and listen to practical insights from leading experts.
- Define personal development career paths and hone essential soft skills for IT students.

### Speaker List / Experts
- Industry experts and representatives from **AWS Vietnam** (co-organized with **Swinburne Vietnam**).

### Highlighted Content

#### 1. Overview of Cloud Computing & AI Growth
- Cloud Computing and Artificial Intelligence (AI) are becoming the core underlying infrastructure for most digital products and global solutions.
- Demand for personnel with Cloud & AI skills is growing rapidly, opening up diverse job roles such as Cloud Engineer, Solutions Architect, DevOps Engineer, etc.

#### 2. Practical Experience in the Working Environment at AWS Vietnam
- Learn about the modern, creative corporate culture and international standard working environment at the AWS Vietnam office.
- Observe real-world collaboration workflows between technical teams, solution architects, and business development departments.

#### 3. Career Orientation & Core Skills
- **Technical Expertise:** Actively equip oneself with solid knowledge of Cloud services (AWS Services), system architecture, and security.
- **Soft Skills:** Communication skills, teamwork, problem-solving mindset, and continuous self-learning capability play a key role in career development.

### Lessons Learned
- Broadened real-world perspectives on the overall picture of Cloud Computing and AI industry trends in Vietnam and globally.
- Understood clear expectations from enterprises for fresh graduates: Not only theoretical knowledge but also a proactive attitude, Cloud-Native mindset, and problem-solving skills.
- Recognized the importance of proactively staying updated with new technologies and obtaining international professional certifications (such as AWS Certified Solutions Architect/Cloud Practitioner).

### Application to Work and Study
- **Building a Specific Learning Roadmap:** Focus on studying fundamental Cloud services on AWS (EC2, S3, Lambda, DynamoDB, etc.) and applying them to course capstone projects.
- **Practicing Real-world Scenarios:** Actively join technical workshops and complete practical hands-on labs to solidify Cloud system deployment skills.
- **Honing Soft Skills:** Actively participate in group activities, improve technical English proficiency, and cultivate critical thinking.

### Practical Experience at the Event
- The field trip provided tremendous inspiration and motivation for learning by witnessing firsthand the professional working environment at a world-leading tech enterprise.
- Sharing sessions and Q&A from AWS speakers were very open, directly addressing students' questions regarding career direction and preparation before graduation.
